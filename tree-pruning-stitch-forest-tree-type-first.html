ANSI A300 Pruning Reference

Reduction Cut to Lateral
Shortens a stem to a lateral branch capable of assuming dominance (typically at least 1/3 the diameter of the parent). Reduces height/spread while avoiding heading epicormics.
Removal Cut at Collar
Removes an entire branch at its origin outside the branch bark ridge and collar. Never cut flush against the trunk to allow defense zone compartmentalization.
Heading Cut (Internodal)
Cuts between nodes, destroying apical dominance and stimulating vigorous uncoordinated watersprouts below the cut. Rarely recommended on young shade trees.
