Tree Pruning Planner — Make the Cuts

Original vector tree · digital cuts · selectable bud arrangement · high-vigor growth response
Step 1 — Plan and mark the tree. Start in Freehand markup. Study the tree's structure, identify the leader you want to preserve, and use the colored drawing tools to mark proposed removal (red), reduction (blue), heading (orange), and retention (green) decisions. These marks are a plan only; they do not remove wood.

Step 2 — Make your digital cuts. Switch to Digital cut mode after reviewing your plan. Select a cut type, then click the branch to remove or shorten wood. For reduction, select a blue lateral junction; for removal, choose a branch near its attachment. Use Retain — identify central leader to click START and STOP on your proposed leader path; this is your own choice, not an automatic answer key.

Step 3 — Predict and reveal one growing season. Complete your cuts, then select Step 3 · One-year response. Select Alternate or Opposite buds under Growth settings. The green shoots follow the surviving branches under a high-vigor model with exaggerated 2× extension for visibility. Change the bud setting in Step 3 to compare responses without changing your cuts. Step 4 — Explain and export. Record your reasoning and export your tree or decision record. Illustrative structural-pruning exercise only: always evaluate branch collars, lateral size, and cut placement on real trees.
